# ESP32-2424S012C (1.28″ round, ESP32-C3) — hardware reference

Board directory `2424s012`. Everything an agent needs to develop Piano
Coach for this board without the vendor material. Labels used below:

* no label — the firmware drives it and it works on the bench units;
* **(schematic, not used by firmware)** — only from the vendor schematic
  or spec, never exercised by Piano Coach;
* **unknown** — not established by any source in the repo.

## 1. Summary

A watch-sized round board from Shenzhen Jingcai Intelligent (JCZN, the
"Sunton"-style ESP32-xxxxSxxx family): an ESP32-C3-MINI-1U module
(single-core 160 MHz RISC-V, 400 KB SRAM, 4 MB in-package flash, no
PSRAM, Wi-Fi + BLE 5), a 1.28″ 240×240 round IPS panel with a GC9A01
controller on SPI, a CST816D capacitive touch controller on I²C (the "C"
SKU; the "N" SKU has no touch), RST and BOOT buttons, a USB-C connector
wired to the C3's native USB (USB Serial/JTAG), a Li-ion charger with a
JST 1.25 battery connector and a slide switch, and a 4-pin SH1.0 UART
header. There is no microSD, no USB host, no speaker and no user LED.
Piano Coach runs its **mini** UI here: songs come from a song pack in
flash, the piano is reached over BLE only.

Notes:

* **No free GPIOs except GPIO8** (and GPIO20/21 on the UART header).
  Every other pin the module exposes is taken.
* **Strapping pins** (module datasheet §3.3, Table 4): GPIO2, GPIO8 and
  GPIO9 are all wired here. GPIO9 low at reset = download boot (that is
  the BOOT button); GPIO2 must be 1 for both boot modes (it is the LCD
  D/C line, nothing pulls it low at reset); GPIO8 must be 1 for download
  boot (R14 pulls it up); GPIO8 = 0 together with GPIO9 = 0 is invalid.
  Do not add anything that holds GPIO2 or GPIO8 low at reset.
* Deep-sleep wake on the C3 is possible only from GPIO0–5 (comment in
  `mod.rs`), which is why BOOT (GPIO9) cannot wake the board and touch
  (GPIO0) can.
* LCD connector J3 (16 pins, schematic): 1 GND, 2 LED−, 3 LED+, 4 VDD
  (3.3 V), 5 GND, 6 GND, 7 D/C, 8 CS, 9 SCL, 10 SDA, 11 REST, 12 GND,
  13 TP_RST, 14 TP_SCL, 15 TP_SDA, 16 TP_INT. Where LED+ connects is not
  legible in the schematic PNG (LED− goes through R8 3.9 Ω to Q2's drain).

## 4. Subsystems

### Power and battery

(schematic, not used by firmware)

* USB VBUS → D1 (1N5819W Schottky) → `5V` → U2 VIN. U2 is a
  charger / power-path IC with LED1–3 (unconnected), SW (to inductor L1
  → BAT+), BAT, KEY and VOUT; its part number is not given. U2 VOUT
  (`VOUT-BAT`) feeds U4, a buck regulator (part number not given) that
  makes `3.3V` for the module, LCD and touch. So the board runs from U2's
  output both on USB and on battery.
* Battery: P2, JST 1.25 mm 2-pin; the spec claims charge/discharge with
  overcharge and overcurrent protection.
* SW1 (the "SWITCH" on the PCB edge) connects U2's KEY pin to GND through
  R10. What KEY does depends on U2: **unknown**.
* No battery-voltage divider to any GPIO is shown: the firmware cannot
  read the battery level, and does not try.
* No power latch: `CAPS.power_latch = false`. "Sleep" in Settings is
  deep sleep (`power_off()`): backlight 0, 300 ms delay, wake on GPIO0
  low, `esp_deep_sleep_start()`. The panel and touch stay powered (no
  switchable rail); the touch controller's INT (GPIO0) pulses low on a
  touch and is the configured wake source. RESET also wakes it. (Wake by
  touch is documented behaviour, not recorded as tested — see §7.)

### USB

* USB-C receptacle: D+/D− on both orientations (A6/B6 = D+, A7/B7 = D−)
  to GPIO19/GPIO18, the C3's USB Serial/JTAG. It is a device port only.
* CC1 (A5) and CC2 (B5) are **unconnected** on the schematic (no 5.1 kΩ
  pull-downs drawn). Whether a C-to-C cable from a USB-C-only supply
  powers the board is **unknown**; the bench uses USB-A-to-C to the PC.
* Spec claims "one-click download": the USB Serial/JTAG peripheral lets
  espflash reset the chip into download mode with no buttons, as long as
  the running firmware keeps the USB Serial/JTAG enabled (Piano Coach
  does: it is the console).

Writes: Slint renders the dirty region line by line; `SpiLcd::draw`
byte-swaps the band to big-endian RGB565, calls
`esp_lcd_panel_draw_bitmap` and waits (≤ 100 ms) on a binary semaphore
given by the transfer-done callback, so the band buffer can be reused on
return. The panel is cleared to the background in 4-line bands before
`disp_on`.

**Mirroring differs between units.** The GC9A01 panels on this board
differ in scan direction between production runs. Unit 1
(`40:4c:ca:8d:a9:68`) is right with no mirroring; unit 2
(`68:67:25:6b:92:fc`) shows everything mirrored left-right. The board
therefore provides `LCD_MIRROR = Some(lcd_mirror)`, which calls
`esp_lcd_panel_mirror(panel, on, false)`. `ui_mini.rs` reads NVS bool
`lcdmirror` (namespace `piano`, default false) right after `lcd_init`,
offers **Settings → Mirror display**, and on a change re-mirrors and
repaints the whole window once (`RepaintBufferType::NewBuffer` for one
frame, since the picture already on the panel flips too). Touch is not
changed: the touch layer follows the glass, not the panel's scan. The
vendor demo shows the vendor knew about this: its `CST816D.cpp` carries a
commented-out `*x = 240 - *x` "if your x-axis is reversed".

### Touch

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Controller | CST816D (vendor demo `CST816D.h`, board comments). The firmware never reads a chip-ID register, so the exact variant is not verified | demo, `cst816.rs` |
| I²C | port 0, new `i2c_master` driver, 7-bit address **0x15**, 400 kHz, glitch filter 7, internal pull-ups on (board also has 4.7 kΩ R5/R6 to 3.3 V) | `cst816.rs`, schematic |
| Pins | SDA 4, SCL 5, RST 1, INT 0 | `mod.rs`, schematic, demo |
| Init | RST low 10 ms, high, wait 60 ms; open bus; write `0xFE = 0xFF` (disable automatic low-power mode — it stops answering otherwise). Failure is not fatal: "touch: CST816D not found … — BOOT key only" | `cst816.rs`, `mod.rs` |
| Read | polled once per UI frame: `transmit_receive` reg 0x02, 5 bytes: `b[0] & 0x0f` = finger count, X = `(b[1] & 0x0f) << 8 \| b[2]`, Y = `(b[3] & 0x0f) << 8 \| b[4]` (12 bits each), 20 ms timeout | `cst816.rs` |
| Mapping | raw X/Y are panel pixels with no transform; points outside 0–239 are dropped | `mod.rs` `touch_point` |
| Gestures | computed in software, not from the chip (`ui_mini.rs` `Gesture`): movement < 14 px and < 700 ms = tap (sent to Slint as press/release at that point); horizontal swipe with dx > 40 px = Back; vertical swipe = scroll, about one row per 40 px (at least one); left swipe does nothing | `ui_mini.rs` |
| INT | not used while running; used as the deep-sleep wake source (GPIO0, low level) | `mod.rs` |

Vendor demo differences (not used by firmware): the demo drives INT as an
output (high 1 ms, low 1 ms) before resetting the chip, waits 300 ms
after reset, uses the Arduino `Wire` default clock, and reads register
0x01 as a gesture code (0x00 none, 0x01 slide down, 0x02 slide up, 0x03
slide left, 0x04 slide right, 0x05 single tap, 0x0B double tap, 0x0C long
press; the demo keeps only up/down). The firmware's shorter 60 ms reset
wait and plain polling work on both bench units.

### Buttons

* **BOOT** (GPIO9, active low, R2 10 kΩ pull-up plus the internal pull-up
  the firmware enables): the single user key. `board::key::Key` debounces
  (25 ms) and decodes tap (reported after the 280 ms double-tap window),
  double tap, and hold (≥ 600 ms). Menus: tap = next row, double tap =
  previous row, hold = open/OK. Play screen: tap = pause/resume (restart
  when finished), double tap = restart, hold = back to the song menu.
  Holding BOOT while pressing RST enters the ROM download mode.
* **RST** on EN: resets the chip and the LCD; wakes from deep sleep.
* `CAPS.buttons = true`, `key_hint = ""`, `wake_hint = "a touch or RESET"`.

### Storage (song pack)

* No microSD (`HAS=` is empty). Songs live in the `songs` data partition
  (subtype `undefined`, 0x170000 = 1472 KiB), written separately from the
  app by `tools/songs.sh 2424s012 flash`. The firmware memory-maps the
  partition (`esp_partition_mmap`, no RAM for the index), validates the
  index CRC, and inflates one song at a time into an exact-size heap
  buffer with a heap-allocated decompressor (miniz_oxide's helpers build
  an 11 KB state on the stack otherwise). Pack songs are `/pack/<index>`.
* `SONGPACK_ARGS="--max-bytes 1400K --max-notes 1200 --prefer 儿时回忆（41）
  --prefer 红色歌谣（52） --prefer 影视剧场（240）"`: 1.4 MB budget, the
  children's, revolutionary and film categories first, then others, and
  only songs with ≤ 1200 notes. Result on the bench: **776 songs, 1.33 MB**
  (`glyphs.slint`: 776 songs, 1344 distinct characters).
* Why 1200 notes: a song's parsed data plus engine state must fit next to
  Bluetooth and the UI in the C3's ~150 KB free heap (measured: 155 KB
  idle, 88 KB free with a 761-note song loaded, 40 KB low-water).
  `piano-core/examples/memprobe.rs` prints a song's heap needs. Loading a
  1200-note song takes ~1.4 s (no FPU).
* `boards/2424s012/glyphs.slint` is generated by `tools/songs.sh 2424s012
  build` (every character the mini UI can draw for this pack); rebuild
  the firmware when it changes.
* Settings and best scores: NVS partition `nvs` (namespace `piano`, i32
  values; bools 0/1). NimBLE bonds are also persisted there
  (`CONFIG_BT_NIMBLE_NVS_PERSIST=y`).

### Radio

* BLE only, native controller + NimBLE host, **central + observer** roles,
  one connection, 2 bonds, 4 CCCDs, preferred ATT MTU 260, security off,
  2 GATT procedures, 12 msys-1 blocks, 12 ACL buffers, controller max 3
  activities (`CONFIG_BT_CTRL_BLE_MAX_ACT=3`). Wi-Fi is never started.
* `bt_controller_start` is a no-op (the controller starts inside
  `nimble_port_init`).
* `ble_relaxed` is **not** set for this board (build.rs sets it only for
  headless boards and `MCU=esp32`), so the link keeps the piano's own
  10 ms interval / 500 ms supervision and `WRITE_GAP = 0` (no batching).
* NimBLE host flow control: off — the generated sdkconfig of the
  2026-10-16 build has `# CONFIG_BT_NIMBLE_HS_FLOW_CTRL is not set`
  (ESP-IDF turns it on by default only for the original ESP32).
* TX power: the firmware raises it to +9 dBm only on the original ESP32
  (`cfg(esp32)`); the C3 runs at the ESP-IDF default.
* Antenna: all vendor documents name the **ESP32-C3-MINI-1U**, which the
  Espressif datasheet defines as the variant with an external antenna
  connector (the MINI-1 has a PCB antenna). Whether the bench units carry
  an antenna connector, a fitted antenna, or a PCB-antenna module is
  **unknown**; no RSSI figures are recorded for this board.

## 6. Build, flash, console

```bash
cd firmware/piano-coach
tools/songs.sh 2424s012 build                       # target/2424s012/songs.bin + boards/2424s012/glyphs.slint
tools/board.sh 2424s012 build --release             # first build compiles ESP-IDF (10+ min): run in background
tools/songs.sh 2424s012 flash --port /dev/ttyACM0   # espflash write-bin --chip esp32c3 … 0x290000 songs.bin
tools/board.sh 2424s012 run --release -- --port /dev/ttyACM0   # flash app (+ bootloader, partition table) and monitor; blocks
```

Direct espflash (what the sessions used), with
`T=target/2424s012/riscv32imc-esp-espidf/release`:

```bash
espflash flash --chip esp32c3 --port P --baud 921600 \
  --bootloader T/bootloader.bin --partition-table T/partition-table.bin T/piano-coach
```

(If the `.bin` files are not next to the ELF, `flash.sh` looks in
`T/build/`.) Flashing the app keeps NVS and the song pack; the pack and
the app are written independently.

**Identify the port.** Several bench boards enumerate as `303a:1001`
(Tab5, T-Display-S3, this board), so VID:PID is not enough. Use the
serial string, which carries the MAC: `udevadm info -q property -n
/dev/ttyACMx | grep ID_SERIAL`, or `espflash board-info --port
/dev/ttyACMx` (chip type, revision, MAC), or the stable
`/dev/serial/by-id/…` path. Unit 1 = `40:4c:ca:8d:a9:68`, unit 2 =
`68:67:25:6b:92:fc`.

**Download mode.** Normally automatic (espflash resets the C3 through the
USB Serial/JTAG). If a firmware has disabled or taken over the USB port:
hold BOOT, press and release RST, release BOOT; `/dev/ttyACM*` then
belongs to the ROM loader. Press RST (or `espflash reset`) to run the app.
The vendor's user manual (p.10) says the board must be power-cycled
before every re-upload; that applies to its Arduino setup. With Piano
Coach, which keeps the USB Serial/JTAG console, espflash resets the C3
into download mode by itself.

**Console.** `espflash monitor --port /dev/ttyACMx` works (it resets the
board once). The log is the USB Serial/JTAG; UART0 on the SH1.0 header
is not the console.

> **Warning (bench experience, earlier session):** opening this board's
> USB Serial/JTAG port with pyserial left the C3 in **download mode**
> (the app stopped; the ROM loader waited). Recover with
> `espflash reset --port /dev/ttyACMx`. The likely cause is the DTR/RTS
> line-state changes on open, which the USB Serial/JTAG peripheral maps
> to reset/BOOT; the AGENTS.md advice "DTR/RTS false" is written for
> USB-UART bridges. Prefer `espflash monitor`; if a script must read the
> port, check afterwards that the app is running.

**Set a pref from the PC** (e.g. a unit too mirrored to navigate). Build
an NVS image and write it at the `nvs` offset:

```csv
key,type,encoding,value
piano,namespace,,
lcdmirror,data,i32,1
```

```bash
IDF=/home/nws/w/rust/herdline/clients/tab5/.embuild/espressif/esp-idf/v5.5.3   # the shared v5.5.3 checkout
python3 $IDF/components/nvs_flash/nvs_partition_generator/nvs_partition_gen.py generate prefs.csv nvs.bin 0x6000
espflash write-bin --port /dev/ttyACMx 0x9000 nvs.bin
```

This **replaces all saved prefs, best scores and BLE bonds** on the unit
(this is how unit 2 got `lcdmirror = 1` on 2026-10-16).

**PC preview of the UI:** `tests/mini_preview` renders the round screens
(`cargo +stable run --release --target x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`).

## 7. Piano Coach support

* UI class **mini** (`UI=mini`, `cfg(ui_mini)`): wheel menu (library,
  Settings), song menu (Learn / Follow / Listen, hands, tempo), play
  screen (next keys in red, ✓/✕ counts, time, ring-of-dots progress),
  results with stars; Settings: easy mode, hint off on hit,
  accompaniment, follow lead time, brightness, Light all keys, All LEDs
  off, about, **Mirror display** (this board only), Sleep. Boots into
  Follow with a random 儿时回忆 song that starts when the piano connects.
* Input: BOOT tap / double / hold, touch (tap rows, swipe up/down to
  scroll, swipe right = back, tap the play screen = pause/resume), and
  the piano-key remote (`piano_core::controls`).
* Transport: BLE to `GZUT-MIDI` (`80 80` header quirk handled in
  `ble_midi.rs`). Only one BLE central can hold the piano: power off the
  Tab5 / tablets / other boards when testing.

## 8. Quirks and lessons learned

* **Panels differ between units** in scan direction: never hard-code a
  mirror; use the `lcdmirror` pref (Settings, or an NVS image from the
  PC). Touch follows the glass and needs no flip.
* **LCD reset is the chip reset** (J3 REST = EN): there is no LCD reset
  GPIO; pass `reset_gpio_num = -1`.
* **The D/C net is named `SPIMISO`** on the schematic (module pin 5,
  GPIO2) but goes to the LCD's D/C pin; there is no MISO. Don't
  configure GPIO2 as MISO.
* **CST816D falls asleep** and stops answering on I²C unless register
  0xFE is set to 0xFF after reset (`cst816.rs` comment: "it stops
  answering in its auto low-power mode").
* **BOOT cannot wake deep sleep** (GPIO9 is not an RTC GPIO on the C3);
  touch (GPIO0) or RESET does.
* **RAM, not flash, is the limit**: no framebuffer, 10-line bands,
  1200-note cap, lean NimBLE pools, IRAM functions moved to flash, Wi-Fi
  never started. Check `heap free … min …` in the log after changes to
  shared code.
* **Flash is tight too**: the app is 2.3 MB of a 2.5 MB partition with
  Slint and the app built at `opt-level="s"`.
* **FatFs LFN must stay on** in sdkconfig even with no SD card (the
  shared ESP-IDF checkout's exFAT patch does not compile without it).
* miniz_oxide's inflate helpers put an 11 KB state on the stack — the
  firmware heap-allocates the decompressor; keep it that way.
* **pyserial on the USB Serial/JTAG port** can leave the C3 in download
  mode — use `espflash monitor`, recover with `espflash reset`.
* All three C3 strapping pins are wired (GPIO2 LCD D/C, GPIO8 pad with
  pull-up, GPIO9 BOOT); keep them high at reset.
* The vendor folder's `5-Schematic/ESP32-C3-MINI-1U.jpg` is **not** this
  board: it is a pinout of a different ESP32-C3 dev board (IMU at 0x68,
  I²C on GPIO8/10, LED on GPIO7). Ignore it for pins.
